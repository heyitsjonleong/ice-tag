How to Play

  • On your turn, spin to determine your move: White, Blue, Anything, or Skip Turn.
  • Light blue hexagons count as either colour!
  • Move exactly one adjacent space per turn (no jumping).
  • You cannot move onto obstacles (polar bears, walruses, holes, glaciers).
  • You must move if an available move exists.
  • Tagging: Move onto a player's space to tag them out! Click their Tag Out button on the right panel when they are caught.
  • Victory: Be the last person left on the board!
